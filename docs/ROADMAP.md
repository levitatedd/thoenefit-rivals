# Roadmap

The canonical backlog is [GitHub Issues](../../issues). This document summarizes phases, not individual tasks.

## Next

- Bootstrap a Windows-friendly Expo/TypeScript app with a working simulator-data dashboard.
- Build and unit-test an isolated, configurable scoring engine.
- Create a sample competition and leaderboard without real health data.

## After prototype

- Integrate Apple HealthKit and Android Health Connect in development builds.
- Validate actual active-calorie and step totals across Apple Watch and Pixel Watch.
- Implement secure accounts, private competitions, invitations, and trophies.

## Later

- Add native watch companions, more wearables, notifications, and richer achievements.

Scoring choices remain intentionally open until tested with sample competitions and users.
